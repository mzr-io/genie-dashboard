Exploration A

Source → Slots → Preview

Three side-by-side panes put the five questions in reading order: what the API sent (left), what the block needs (middle), and what the result looks like (right). The middle checklist and the top status bar answer "what's mapped / what's missing" at a glance. Admins can drag fields onto slots or pick them from a list. Presentation options open inline on one slot at a time.

Trade-off: best when admins know their API's shape and map precisely, field by field / weaker when the response is large or deeply nested, because the tree gets long and three panes leave less room for each one.
