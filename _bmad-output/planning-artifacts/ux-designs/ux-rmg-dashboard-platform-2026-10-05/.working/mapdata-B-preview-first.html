Exploration B

Click the preview to map

The block itself is the mapping surface. Every region of the live preview is a dashed hotspot named after its slot. Clicking a hotspot opens a field picker that lists only compatible fields and explains, in one line, why the others are unavailable. Admins see the result immediately. A small checklist on the side and a tray of API fields at the bottom cover "what's missing" and "what came from the API".

Trade-off: best for first-time or occasional admins who think in "I want this number there", and for spotting the wrong field because it looks wrong / weaker when slots have no visible region (Data-as-of, chart series 2..n) or when admins need to review the whole mapping as a list.
