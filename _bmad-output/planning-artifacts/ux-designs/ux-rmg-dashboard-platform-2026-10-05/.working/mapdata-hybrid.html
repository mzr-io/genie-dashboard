The admin sets semantic roles on real sample rows (C), and Dashflow fills the KPI & Chart slots from those roles. The slot checklist (A) is where the admin reviews and overrides each slot. It shows every slot, its status and confidence, and its presentation controls. Clicking a region of the live preview (B) jumps to that slot's field picker. The data in this render came from Paste sample JSON, so a persistent badge says it is used only for mapping and preview.
