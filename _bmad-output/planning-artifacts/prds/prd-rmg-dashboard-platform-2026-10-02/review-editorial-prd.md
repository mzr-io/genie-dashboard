# Editorial review: PRD, RMG Dynamic Dashboard Platform

**Purpose read.** This document exists to help the delivery team, client stakeholders and downstream BMAD workflows agree on and build exactly what the first release of the RMG Dynamic Dashboard Platform must do, without rediscovering requirements.

**Structure model.** Strategic/Context (Pyramid), with a Reference/Database body (§4 FRs, §5 NFRs, §13 Open Questions). Judged against: top-down headline, logical grouping, most critical first, MECE groups, consistent item schema.

**Overall.** The document is well organized, carefully tagged and largely free of redundancy. Most findings are about consistency: Glossary terms and Role names used in lowercase or as synonyms, acceptance-criteria labels that vary, cross-references that are incomplete or slightly wrong, and missing keys for codes. No requirement, ID, number, status tag or Glossary term is changed by any row. The Open Questions, phase-gating table and Assumptions Index are preserved in full.

**Legend.** Class: **SAFE** = mechanical, meaning-preserving fix the author can accept without discussion. **JUDGMENT** = needs an author decision (adds scaffolding, resolves an ambiguity in a way that could shift meaning, or applies a house-style choice across the document). Structure tags: CUT, MERGE, MOVE, CONDENSE, QUESTION, PRESERVE.
