## 2. KPI entries

Abbreviations for module dependency: PROD = production, QA = quality, HR = workforce/HR/attendance, IE = IE/engineering (SMV database), PLAN = planning, ORD = orders, INV = inventory, SHIP = shipment.

**On the 30-second refresh judgement:** this is reasoning from the capture methods documented in #48–#52, not a sourced claim. Manual hourly boards (#48) cap freshness at about 1 hour. Bundle or operator scanning (#49, #52) and machine IoT (#51) can support near-real-time updates. No source gave a measured latency.

### K1. SMV / SAM (standard minute value / standard allowed minute)
- **Aliases:** SMV, SAM, standard time, standard minutes. "SAH" is the hours form (#14).
- **Definition:** standard time for a qualified worker to perform an operation. Garment SMV = Σ operation SMVs (#1, #2, #37).
- **Formula variants:**
  - Time study: (observed time × rating) + allowances (#4, #6).
  - PMTS/GSD: Σ predetermined motion-code times (#37).
  - SMV-vs-SAM split: SMV excludes allowances and SAM = SMV + allowance % (#7, low confidence). Other sources treat the two terms as interchangeable (#3).
- **Source data:** operation breakdown per style, observed cycle times, performance rating, allowance policy, or GSD motion analysis.
- **Granularity:** operation × style. Rolled up to garment/style.
- **30-second refresh:** not applicable. It is master data, changed only when a style or method changes.
- **Period:** static per style or version.
- **Stored or calculated:** **stored** (master data), even though it is derived offline by IE.
- **Module:** IE (SMV database). Consumed by PLAN, PROD and costing.
- **Data-quality prerequisites:** one consistent method (#3), a documented allowance policy (#5), versioning per style and method change.
- **Decision supported:** costing and pricing, capacity planning, targets. It is the denominator basis for every efficiency KPI.
- **Level:** used by management indirectly. The SMV itself is engineering-level.

### K2. Line efficiency (sewing line efficiency)
- **Aliases:** line efficiency %, sewing efficiency, overall efficiency, actual efficiency.
- **Definition:** standard minutes produced divided by minutes attended (spent) by the line's manpower.
- **Formula variants:**
  - (Output × SMV) / (Manpower × working hours × 60) × 100 (#8, #18, #33).
  - Standard minutes earned / actual minutes attended (#27).
  - Achieved production / target production at attended minutes (#27, alternative).
  - **Overall** (includes off-standard and lost time) vs **on-standard** (excludes them) (#23, #35).
  - Manpower = operators only (#18 wording "operators"), or operators + helpers (#15, #33). Some sources also include ironmen (snippet example in search: 32 operators + 4 helpers + 2 ironmen; not fetched).
  - Output = QC-passed pieces only, or all stitched pieces (#16, #39).
- **Source data:** line output count (pieces per hour/day), style SMV, headcount present per line (by role), working minutes (shift + OT), and, for on-standard, off-standard/lost-time minutes with reason codes.
- **Granularity:** line × hour, rolled up to shift/day. Piece or bundle counts at line-out.
- **30-second refresh:** realistic **only** if output is captured by line-out scanning/RFID or IoT counters (#49, #50) and attendance is known at shift start (#15). With a manual hourly board (#48), hourly is the best possible. The denominator (attended minutes) needs live headcount, including people moved between lines.
- **Period:** intra-day (hourly) for supervisors. Reported as shift/day, and trended weekly/monthly.
- **Stored or calculated:** **calculated** (from stored output, SMV, attendance and time).
- **Module:** PROD + IE + HR (attendance) + QA (if QC-pass output is used).
- **Data-quality prerequisites:** accurate SMV per style (K1), attendance captured before or at shift start, line-transfer tracking (#15), hourly piece counts, a defined output definition, and lost-time capture with reasons for the on-standard variant.
- **Decision supported:** line performance management, incentives (#24), comparing lines, intervention. Line level, and a key management metric.
- **Level:** shop floor (hourly) and management (daily per line).

### K3. Factory / plant / floor efficiency
- **Aliases:** factory efficiency %, overall factory efficiency.
- **Definition:** efficiency aggregated across lines.
- **Formula variants:**
  - Σ SAH / Σ hours × 100, weighted (#13).
  - Total minutes produced × 100 / total minutes attended (#40).
  - Simple average of line efficiencies is mentioned as the contrast, not recommended (#13).
- **Source data:** as K2, for all lines.
- **Granularity:** factory/floor × day. Hourly is possible.
- **30-second refresh:** same constraints as K2. Feasible only with automated output and attendance capture.
- **Period:** daily. Trended weekly/monthly.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated. It must be computed from summed numerators and denominators, not from stored line percentages.
- **Module:** PROD + IE + HR.
- **Data-quality prerequisites:** as K2, plus no double-counting of transferred staff (#15).
- **Decision supported:** an executive headline for factory productivity, cost competitiveness and capacity planning baselines.
- **Level:** executive/CXO and management.

### K4. Operator efficiency (individual)
- **Aliases:** operator efficiency, individual efficiency, operator performance.
- **Definition:** minutes produced by the operator / minutes worked (#18). The on-standard variant excludes off-standard time (#23).
- **Source data:** pieces per operator per operation, operation SMV, operator clock-in/out, off-standard time.
- **Granularity:** workstation/operator × operation × hour/bundle.
- **30-second refresh:** requires operator-level scanning (RFID or QR terminal, #49, #52). This is infeasible with end-of-line counting (#48), which cannot attribute output to operators.
- **Period:** daily/shift, plus a skill history.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated.
- **Module:** PROD + IE + HR.
- **Data-quality prerequisites:** operation-level SMVs, per-operator piece counts (bundle tickets or scanning), off-standard logging.
- **Decision supported:** skill matrix (#24), incentive/piece-rate pay, training, line balancing.
- **Level:** shop floor and IE. Not typically executive.

### K5. Target vs actual output (achievement %), hourly target, daily target
- **Aliases:** achievement %, plan vs actual, hourly target, daily line target, target efficiency.
- **Definition and formulas:**
  - Hourly target at 100% = 60 / operation SAM (#12).
  - Daily line target = shift hours × 60 × operators × planned efficiency / garment SAM (#17).
  - Line capacity = manpower × minutes × target efficiency / SMV (#9).
  - Achievement % = actual / target × 100 (#27 alternative, #33).
  - The target efficiency is often set around 75% (#46).
  - Learning-curve-adjusted day-wise targets apply for new styles (#47).
- **Source data:** SMV, planned efficiency (from PLAN or the learning curve), manpower, hours, and actual hourly output.
- **Granularity:** line × hour. Day.
- **30-second refresh:** the target is static per hour. The actual side has the same constraints as K2. Hourly target vs actual is a common feature of scanning systems (#52).
- **Period:** hourly (floor) and daily.
- **Stored or calculated:** the target is calculated (often stored once set). The actual is stored (counts). The percentage is calculated.
- **Module:** PROD + PLAN + IE.
- **Data-quality prerequisites:** an agreed planned efficiency per line/style and a learning-curve policy.
- **Decision supported:** real-time intervention (floor), daily performance review (management).
- **Level:** shop floor (hourly) and management (daily).

### K6. Produced minutes vs available minutes (SAH earned vs hours available)
- **Aliases:** standard minutes earned, produced minutes, SAH, available minutes, attended minutes, man-minutes.
- **Definition:** produced = Σ(pieces × SMV) (#14, #27, #42). Available/attended = manpower × working minutes (#8). The ratio of the two is efficiency.
- **Granularity:** line/factory × hour/day.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated from stored counts, SMV and attendance.
- **Module:** PROD + IE + HR.
- **30-second refresh:** as K2.
- **Decision supported:** a common currency for comparing different styles and lines. The basis for capacity and cost-per-minute (#44).
- **Level:** management.

### K7. Labour productivity and machine productivity
- **Aliases:** pieces per operator, pieces per machine, garments per manpower.
- **Formulas:**
  - Line output / total manpower (#19, #34).
  - Line output / machines (#19, #34).
  - "Output per machine per operator" (#30).
  - A minutes-based variant (produced minutes per operator) is implied by K6 but is **unverified** as a named KPI.
- **Source data:** output, headcount (with a defined manpower scope), machine count.
- **Granularity:** line/factory × day.
- **30-second refresh:** possible with automated counts, but the metric is meaningful daily. It is not comparable across styles of different SMV (this caveat is reasoning, not sourced).
- **Period:** daily/monthly.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated.
- **Module:** PROD + HR + (machine register) IE/maintenance.
- **Decision supported:** manpower planning, benchmarking.
- **Level:** management/executive (monthly).

### K8. Capacity utilisation
- **Formulas:**
  - Produced minutes / capacity minutes × 100, where capacity = operators × efficiency × hours (#42).
  - Production capacity in pieces = available minutes / garment SAM (#21).
- **Source data:** booked or produced standard minutes, planned capacity (manpower, hours, planned efficiency).
- **Granularity:** factory/floor/line × week/month.
- **30-second refresh:** not meaningful. It is a planning metric.
- **Period:** weekly/monthly.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated.
- **Module:** PLAN + ORD + IE + HR.
- **Data-quality prerequisites:** SMV on every order, planned efficiency assumptions.
- **Decision supported:** order booking, subcontracting, overtime, capacity investment.
- **Level:** executive.

### K9. Machine utilisation
- **Formulas (two forms):**
  - Running time × 100 / time available (#20).
  - (Running time per piece × pieces) × 100 / time available (#28).
- **Source data:** needle-running time (IoT sensors) or a per-piece run-time estimate, plus available time.
- **Granularity:** machine × shift.
- **30-second refresh:** realistic with machine IoT (#51). Not feasible manually.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated (from stored sensor time series).
- **Module:** PROD/maintenance.
- **Decision supported:** machine investment and allocation.
- **Level:** shop floor and IE.

### K10. Sewing OEE
- **Formulas:**
  - Availability × Performance × Quality (#29, #31).
  - Garment simplification: ((output − defects) × SMV) × 100 / (shift hours × operators × 60) (#31). This is essentially line efficiency with QC-passed output, which shows that the term overlaps with K2.
- **Caveat:** labour-intensive sewing needs an adapted OEE (#32).
- **Source data:** planned time, downtime with reasons, output, SMV, defects.
- **Granularity:** line or machine × shift.
- **30-second refresh:** possible with IoT and scanning (#51). The quality term needs inline QC capture.
- **Period:** shift/daily.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated.
- **Module:** PROD + QA + maintenance + IE.
- **Benchmark:** 85% "world class" is a generic manufacturing figure (#53, low).
- **Level:** management. Its adoption in RMG is less universal than efficiency, but the extent is **unverified**.

### K11. Lost time / downtime % and off-standard time
- **Aliases:** NPT (non-productive time), lost time %, downtime %, off-standard time.
- **Definition:** time attended but not spent on standard work, with reasons such as waiting, breakdown, power failure, line setting, meetings and missing trims (#25, #40, #23).
- **Source data:** start/stop events with reason codes, per line/operator.
- **Granularity:** line/operator × event.
- **30-second refresh:** feasible with terminal logging (#49) or IoT (#51). Manual logs are entered after the event.
- **Period:** shift/daily.
- **Stored or calculated:** events are stored. The percentage is calculated.
- **Module:** PROD + maintenance + INV (trims shortages) + PLAN.
- **Decision supported:** root-cause removal. The impact can be large (#26).
- **Level:** management and floor.

### K12. Style changeover time and the learning curve
- **Definition:** time between the last piece of the old style and the first piece of the new style (#40). The learning curve is the efficiency ramp after a changeover (#47).
- **Source data:** timestamps of the last and first piece per line, daily efficiency per style-day.
- **Granularity:** line × changeover event.
- **30-second refresh:** event-driven, so not needed.
- **Period:** per event, trended monthly.
- **Stored or calculated:** timestamps are stored. Duration is calculated.
- **Module:** PROD + PLAN + IE.
- **Decision supported:** planning and style loading, SMED (a ScienceDirect SMED-in-Bangladesh paper was seen as a snippet only).
- **Level:** management.

### K13. Line balancing efficiency and bottleneck
- **Formula:** (bottleneck capacity × SMV) / (manpower × 60) × 100 (#11, low). Peer-reviewed balance-loss and cycle-time formulas could not be retrieved (Wiley 403).
- **Source data:** operation SMVs, operator allocation, per-operation capacity from time study.
- **Granularity:** line × style setup.
- **30-second refresh:** no. It is set at line setup or after a rebalancing study.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated by IE.
- **Module:** IE.
- **Level:** IE/floor.

### K14. Man-to-machine ratio
- **Formula:** total manpower : total sewing machines (#40).
- **Data:** headcount, machine register.
- **Granularity:** factory/line × month.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated.
- **Module:** HR + IE.
- **Level:** executive (benchmarking overhead/indirect labour).

### K15. Cutting KPIs
- **Marker efficiency:** marker area used by parts / total marker area × 100, with 80–85% acceptable (#45).
- **Fabric utilisation %** (#45).
- **Marked vs achieved consumption** (#45).
- **Cut-to-ship ratio**, in conflicting forms: cut/ship with target 1 (#40), or ship/cut × 100 with ≈98% good (#41).
- **Cutting output:** pieces or garments cut per day (no formula source retrieved; **unverified**).
- **Source data:** marker files (CAD), lay records, fabric issued/returned, cut qty, shipped qty.
- **Granularity:** cut order/lay; order.
- **30-second refresh:** not meaningful (per-lay and per-order events).
- **Period:** per order, and monthly.
- **Stored or calculated:** marker efficiency is stored from CAD. The others are calculated.
- **Module:** PROD (cutting) + INV + SHIP + ORD.
- **Level:** cut-to-ship and fabric utilisation are executive (cost). Marker efficiency is department level.

### K16. Finishing output
- **Status:** no finishing-specific KPI definition was retrieved. Finishing efficiency is likely the same SMV-based formula applied to iron/pack operations, but this is an **unverified belief**.

### K17. Plan Performance Index, overtime %, labour cost per minute/unit
- **Formulas:**
  - PPI = planned work done / planned × 100 (#43).
  - OT % (#44).
  - Cost per minute (#44).
  - Labour cost per unit (#22).
- **Granularity:** factory × week/month.
- **30-second refresh:** no (these depend on payroll).
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated.
- **Module:** PLAN, HR/payroll, finance.
- **Level:** executive.

### K18. Throughput rate
- **Formula:** output / time period (#30).
- **Granularity:** line × hour.
- **30-second refresh:** feasible with automated counting.
- **Stored or calculated:** calculated.
- **Level:** floor.

## 3. Formula and terminology variations

1. **SMV vs SAM.** One view holds that they are interchangeable, or that SAM is used for planning and SMV for costing (#3). The other holds that SMV excludes allowances and SAM = SMV + allowance (#7, low, snippet). Meanwhile #4 and #6 compute "SMV" and "SAM" with the same allowance-inclusive formula. **These conflict and are not resolved.**
2. **SMV derivation.** Time study (observed × rating + allowances, #4, #6) vs PMTS/GSD motion codes (#37). The two give different values for the same operation (this is a reasonable inference, not quantified by any retrieved source).
3. **Allowance structure.** S2 lists personal/fatigue, machine delay and bundle handling (#4, #5) and says the percentages are factory policy. #6 uses a single "Allowance" term.
4. **Line efficiency denominator: lost time.** Overall efficiency includes off-standard and lost time. On-standard efficiency excludes them (#23, #35).
5. **Line efficiency denominator: manpower scope.** Operators only (#18) vs operators + helpers (#15, #33). Ironmen are sometimes included (snippet only).
6. **Line efficiency numerator: output definition.** QC-passed pieces vs all stitched pieces (#16, #39).
7. **Efficiency as minutes ratio vs output ratio.** Minutes ratio (#8, #27) vs achieved/target production (#27 alternative). The two are equivalent only if the target is set at 100% efficiency on attended minutes.
8. **Factory efficiency aggregation.** Weighted sum (#13) vs averaging line efficiencies (noted as the contrast in #13).
9. **Machine utilisation.** Two forms: #20 vs #28.
10. **OEE.** Classic A × P × Q (#29) vs the garment "simplified" OEE, which collapses into QC-pass line efficiency (#31).
11. **Cut-to-ship ratio.** Cut ÷ shipped with target 1 (#40) vs shipped ÷ cut × 100 with ≈98% good (#41). These are inverse definitions.
12. **Operator capacity.** 3600 / (cycle time + allowance) vs 60 / SMV (#10).

## 4. Glossary

| Term | Definition | Source |
|---|---|---|
| SMV (Standard Minute Value) | Standard time in minutes for an operation or garment. Often allowance-inclusive. Garment SMV = Σ operation SMVs. | #2, #6, #37 |
| SAM (Standard Allowed Minute) | Standard time for a standard worker including allowances. Used interchangeably with SMV, or as SMV + allowance (conflict). | #1, #3, #7 |
| SAH (Standard Allowed Hours) | Output × SAM / 60. Standard hours earned. | #14 |
| Basic time | Observed (average cycle) time × rating / standard rating. | #4 |
| Performance rating | The observer's assessment of operator pace relative to standard, used to normalise observed time. | #4, #6 |
| Allowances | Additions to basic time: personal/fatigue, machine delay, bundle handling. Percentages follow factory policy. | #4, #5 |
| Time study | Direct observation of cycle times plus rating plus allowances to set SMV. | #4, #6 |
| PMTS | Predetermined Motion Time System. Standard time built from predefined motion times. | #37, OCS "PMTS software" page (search result) |
| GSD / GSDCost | General Sewing Data, now Coats Digital GSDCost. A PMTS with 39 motion codes, used to set SMVs, method standards and balancing. | #37 |
| Method standard | A documented breakdown of the motions/method for an operation, the basis of its SMV. | #37 |
| IE (industrial engineering) | The function that sets SMVs, targets and line balancing, and runs efficiency analysis (inferred from S2 author role and S16; generic). | S2, S16 |
| Produced / earned minutes | Pieces × SMV. | #27, #42 |
| Available / attended minutes (man-minutes) | Manpower × working minutes. | #8, #27 |
| Line efficiency | Produced minutes / attended minutes × 100. | #8, #18 |
| Overall efficiency | Efficiency including off-standard and lost time in the denominator. | #23 |
| On-standard efficiency | Efficiency counting only time on standard jobs. | #23 |
| Off-standard time | Time an operator spends on non-standard work or waiting (breakdown, line setting, meeting, no trims). | #23, #25 |
| Lost time / NPT / downtime | Non-productive attended time, usually reason-coded. | #23, #40 |
| Hourly target | 60 / operation SAM (at 100%), or line target per hour at planned efficiency. | #12, #17 |
| Planned / target efficiency | The efficiency assumed for setting targets and capacity, often about 75%. | #17, #46 |
| Line capacity | Manpower × minutes × target efficiency / SMV. | #9 |
| Line balancing | Allocating operations and operators so per-operation capacities are equalised against the bottleneck. | #11, #46 |
| Bottleneck | The operation with the lowest capacity, which limits line output. | #11 |
| Balancing loss | Efficiency lost because operation SAMs cannot be perfectly balanced. | #46 |
| Learning curve | The efficiency ramp-up after a new style is loaded. Used to set day-wise targets. | #47 |
| Style changeover | Switching a line to a new style. Measured as last old piece to first new piece. | #40 |
| Man-to-machine ratio | Total manpower : sewing machines. | #40 |
| Cut-to-ship ratio | Cut qty vs shipped qty (two inverse conventions). | #40, #41 |
| Marker efficiency | Pattern area / marker area × 100. | #45 |
| OEE | Availability × Performance × Quality. | #29, #31 |
| PPI | Plan Performance Index: planned work done / planned. | #43 |
| WIP | Work in progress: bundles inside the line between line-in and line-out. | #49, #50 |
| Bundle | The unit of cut pieces moved through sewing, tracked by ticket/RFID/QR. | #49, #50 |
| Hourly production board | A manual board on which end-of-line output is recorded hourly. | #48 |
| Helper | Non-machine operator in the line. Included in manpower by some sources. | #15, #33 |

## 5. Leads worth chasing

- **Peer-reviewed formulas:** Bongomin et al. 2020, *Engineering Reports* (Wiley, DOI 10.1002/eng2.12157), for line efficiency, balance delay, smoothness index and cycle/takt time. Try the PMC/ResearchGate copy.
- **SAM and efficiency statistics:** AUTEX Research Journal 2019 (DOI 10.2478/aut-2019-0045), "Statistical Analysis of SAM on Sewing Efficiency". It probably quantifies how efficiency varies with SAM; this matters for comparability across styles.
- **Learning curves:** "Empirical Analysis and Modeling of Learning Curve in Apparel Manufacturing" and "Forecasting Accuracy of Learning Curves in Apparel Industry" (ResearchGate).
- **SMED / changeover:** SMED in Bangladeshi garments (ScienceDirect S2666790822001975).
- **Off-standard time:** the first-hour sewing efficiency RCA paper (ResearchGate 365946416), the 14.8%→7.6% off-standard figure.
- **Bangladesh bodies:** BGMEA/BKMEA, and ILO Better Work Bangladesh productivity or "line efficiency" publications. Not found this round. Try "Better Work discussion paper productivity" or "ILO garment productivity Bangladesh".
- **Vendor documentation:** WFX, Tukatech, Datatex, Intellocut, and Coats FastReactPlan. Look at how their MES/ERP define efficiency (with or without NPT, helpers) and their capture latency.
- **OCS cutting and finishing KPI pages:** cutting efficiency, cutting productivity per cutter, finishing efficiency. Not yet fetched.
